## 🚀 About Me (for undergraduate admissions)

I’m a hands-on student who learns by building. My interests sit at the intersection of embedded systems (IoT), robotics, and applied AI. I focus on projects that have measurable, real-world impact — for example, improving accessibility or improving resource use in agriculture. I care deeply about responsible design and about making technical work accessible to non-technical users.

What I bring to a university community:
- Curiosity and sustained learning — currently studying agentic AI frameworks and Large Language Models (LLMs), while applying them to practical problems.
- Project leadership — I have taken roles as Project Lead and Tech Lead on multi-week initiatives that required coordinating design, hardware, and software work.
- Hands-on engineering skills — from sensor integration and microcontrollers to Python, web interfaces, and simple ML experiments.
- Communication & outreach — I write articles to explain learnings and share project case studies publicly.

I prefer honest statements about my abilities: I am learning LLMs and agentic AI, I build IoT and robotics prototypes, and I enjoy writing and teaching others what I learn.
